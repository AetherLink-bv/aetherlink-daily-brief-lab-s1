Scope

  • In scope: synthetic fixtures, the schema, one renderer, one bounded SDK route with one exact read-only tool, a host writer, repeatable proof, a trigger proposal.
  • Out of scope: writing to a source, sending messages, changing tickets, work email, public deployment, storing raw connector output, installing a scheduler.
  • Assumption: the approved Jira connection and the approved Vertex route may not exist in a session. Then live proof stays OPEN.
